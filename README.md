## About Us

**SynLab-OCSA** is the open-source home of the **Organic Catalysis Structural Analysis (OCSA)** group at the [Department of Industrial Chemistry "Toso Montanari"](https://chimica-industriale.unibo.it/it), **Alma Mater Studiorum – University of Bologna**.

We design and study catalytic methods that build complex molecules efficiently and sustainably, and we combine experiment with spectroscopy and computation to determine exactly how those molecules are put together in three dimensions. Our work spans the full chain from reaction design to stereochemical proof.

## Research Focus

- **Asymmetric catalysis & organocatalysis**: selective, metal-free and metal-assisted routes to enantiomerically enriched compounds
- **Sustainable catalytic methodologies**: greener conditions, milder reagents and more atom-economical transformations
- **Structural & stereochemical analysis**: determining configuration and conformation of complex molecules
- **Unconventional chirality**: stereochemical features beyond the classical stereocentre
- **Spectroscopic methods in organic chemistry**: NMR and related techniques applied to real structural problems
- **Computational conformer analysis**: systematic exploration of conformational ensembles, supporting experimental interpretation

## Laboratories & Services

- **Organic Spectroscopy Laboratory**: structural elucidation and stereochemical characterization of organic compounds
- **Consultancy & Services**: analytical and structural support for academic and industrial partners
